Onboarding: New employees complete onboarding during their first week, including HR and IT sessions.
Working Hours: Core hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, Monday to Friday.
Remote Work: Employees may work remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Expenses: Expenses above 5,000 INR need prior manager approval.
Notice Period: The notice period for resignation is 60 days.
