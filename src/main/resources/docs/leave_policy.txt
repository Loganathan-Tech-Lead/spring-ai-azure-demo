Annual Leave: Employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year.
Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical note is needed after 3 consecutive days.
Maternity Leave: Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave.
Paternity Leave: Paternity leave is 2 weeks of paid leave.
Carry Over: Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next year.
